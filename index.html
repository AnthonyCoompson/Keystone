How It Works
From description
to defensible framework

Keystone replaces days of manual logic model review with a structured, AI-assisted workflow that surfaces gaps before they reach a submission.

Name your initiative and set the mandate classification. AI fills the form from a single plain-language sentence — or fill manually in under two minutes.
02
Build the Logic Model

Audit Engine
Seven rules. Zero gaps.

The audit engine runs seven structural checks every time a component is added or edited. Each rule targets a distinct failure mode in policy logic.

High Risk
Dead End
Activities with no mapped Outputs or Outcomes. Resources committed to actions that produce no documented deliverable — making the activity unaccountable and unevaluable.
Medium Risk
Miracle Leap
A major systemic outcome claimed from activities structurally insufficient to produce it. Fires when 60%+ of activities are low-leverage (meetings, webinars, surveys). Severity scales with ratio.
Medium Risk
Blind Spot
An Output or Outcome with no defined verification source. Without a named tracking mechanism, the benchmark cannot be measured, reported on, or audited by Treasury Board.
Medium Risk
Circular Logic
An Outcome that semantically mirrors an Activity — the causal chain loops back rather than progressing. Detected via word overlap analysis across component descriptions.
Advisory
Orphaned Input
An Input whose subject matter appears in no Activity. Resources committed but never consumed by the logic chain — a silent budget accountability gap that auditors will find.
Advisory
Scale Mismatch
An Outcome sets a quantified target but no Activity has a benchmark. Without measurable activity-level commitments, scale of effort cannot be shown to match scale of ambition.
Advisory
Duplicate Component
Two components of the same type with high semantic overlap — redundant entries that inflate the appearance of coverage without adding analytical value to the model.

About Keystone
Built from the
inside of policy work

Keystone was built out of a direct need — the gap between the rigour that Treasury Board and Deputy Ministers expect in policy logic, and the informal, undocumented way most logic models are actually assembled.

Policy analysts shouldn’t spend three days in Word trying to find the hole in a logic chain that an audit engine can flag in three seconds.

Every audit rule reflects a real accountability failure seen in government policy submissions: the activity that goes nowhere, the systemic outcome that no webinar series can deliver, the benchmark that no one knows how to track.
